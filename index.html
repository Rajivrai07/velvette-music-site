Questions, Answered

Kya Velvette Music free hai?
Haan, 100% free. Koi subscription nahi, koi hidden charges nahi — bas download karo aur gaane suno.
Kya sign-up karna zaroori hai?
Nahi. Bina account banaye app chalao. Agar personalised recommendations chahiye to YouTube Music se sign-in kar sakte ho — optional hai.
Mere phone pe chalega?
Android 8.0 ya usse upar ke sabhi phones pe chalega. Do version hain — modern 64-bit phones ke liye arm64 wala, aur purane 32-bit phones ke liye alag wala button neeche diya hai.
Kya gaane background mein chalenge?
Haan! Screen off kar do ya doosra app khol lo — gaana rukega nahi. Notification se play/pause bhi control kar sakte ho.
Kya gaane download kar sakte hain?
Haan. Kisi bhi gaane ya playlist ko offline save karo aur bina internet ke suno — Library > Downloads mein milenge.
Install karte waqt "harmful app" warning aaye to?
Ye Google Play Protect ka warning hai jo Play Store ke bahar download ki gayi har app par aata hai. Velvette Music poori tarah safe hai — bas "Install anyway" dabao.
Update kaise milega?
App mein About > Check for updates se, ya isi website se hamesha latest APK download kar sakte ho.
